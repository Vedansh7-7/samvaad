How it works

See the conversation, not just the argument.

Samvaad reads a real conversation between the two of you and shows you what happened, what changed it, and what you could say instead. Here is exactly how, and what happens to your words.

The journey

From a hard chat to a kinder line

It takes a few minutes, and you choose how you want to see the result.

Bring in the conversationPaste the chat straight from WhatsApp, or upload a recording. Hinglish, Hindi and English all work.
Smart analysis reads itIt looks at who said what, where the tone shifted, and which words turned it.
You get a scoreA clear number for the conversation, with the parts that make it up.
Choose how to see it
Play the reflectionWatch itYour real moment plays back, acted out in your own words, then a kinder version of the same moment.
Read the analysisRead itThe full written report: what went wrong, what went well, and the exact lines to try.
Track it over timeEach conversation adds to your trend, so you can see yourselves getting better.

Your score

What the numbers mean

Every part is out of 100. Higher is better, except heat, where lower is calmer.

OverallHow the conversation went, all things considered.
ConnectionHow much you turned toward each other.
EmpathyHow well each of you heard the other.
HeatHow escalated it got. If it runs high, Samvaad offers a minute to breathe first.
Needs careMixedGoing well

Good to know

Before you start

Three free conversations. Each account gets three during the trial. If a lot of people are reflecting at the same moment, you may wait a few seconds. Nothing is lost while you wait, and it will carry on by itself.
