Privacy at Samvaad.

A wellness product · India DPDP-aligned · data resident in Mumbai (ap-south-1)

What we collect

Only what you give us: the conversation text or audio you submit, your name(s), your email (if you sign in), and — only if you opt in — your WhatsApp number for gentle daily check-ins.

How we use it

To produce your reflection (scores, patterns, kinder scripts, a voiced replay) and, if you opted in, to send one daily check-in. Nothing more. We do not sell your data.

Audio is deleted

Uploaded audio is transcribed and then deleted — we don't keep the recording. Analysis is stored only against your own account.

Your data is yours

Row-Level Security means you only ever see your own rows. You can view or delete your data at any time. We keep records append-only so they can't be silently altered — you stay in control of what exists and what gets erased (your DPDP right to erasure).

Consent-first

For a conversation that includes another person, we ask you to confirm you have their consent. WhatsApp check-ins are opt-in only, to your own number, and you can stop anytime by replying STOP. No under-18 use without parental consent.
