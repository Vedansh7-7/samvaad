What Samvaad is

Samvaad is a wellness tool for reflecting on conversations. You bring in a conversation, and it gives you a score, the patterns it noticed, lines you could try, and a replay. It is made with artificial intelligence, so it can misread things. Use it as a mirror, and trust your own judgement about your relationship.

Your account

You need to be 18 or older, or have a parent's or guardian's consent. Keep your sign-in link and code to yourself. You can also try Samvaad as a guest, in which case nothing is saved.

Other people's words

Most conversations include someone else. Only analyse a conversation when that person has agreed to it. Samvaad asks you to confirm this every time, and you are responsible for that consent being real.

Fair use

  • Do not upload anything illegal, or anything involving a child.
  • Do not use Samvaad, or what it shows you, to monitor, pressure or harm another person.
  • Do not try to break, overload or copy the service, or get around the free limits.

The free trial

Every account starts with three free conversations, and guests get a few each day. We may change these limits. If paid plans arrive, you will always see the price and what you get before you pay.

Your content
