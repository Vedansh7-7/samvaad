# Product

## Register

brand

> Samvaad's deliverable is a *feeling* first — calm, safety, the sense of being
> understood — and only then a tool. The app surface (record → analyze → report →
> intervene) is where people spend their time, but the emotional impression is the
> product. Design leads; the task follows. Hold app-grade rigor on states, a11y, and
> performance *inside* a brand-grade emotional shell.

## Users

People in the Indian market (Hinglish + Devanagari speakers) who want to understand a
conversation — often a hard one. Two arrival contexts shape everything:

- **Relationship** — a couple (two people, consent required) or a solo person describing
  a relationship situation. They want to see the patterns underneath an exchange and get
  exact, kinder lines to try next time.
- **Self / Introspection** — one person reflecting, cooling down, refocusing on
  themselves. The companion mascot **Sathi** reflects back.

Critically, a user may arrive **distressed** — right after an argument, escalated, not
in a state to read a dashboard. The job-to-be-done in that moment is: *help me breathe,
then help me understand, then give me words.* The product meets emotion before insight.

## Product Purpose

Help people understand their conversations and respond better. A user records, uploads, or
pastes a conversation and receives:

1. A **report** — scores, the patterns underneath, what each person did well, and exact
   lines to try next time ("scripts").
2. An **intervene layer**, in the order a person actually needs it:
   - **Breathe** — a guided exercise (inhale → hold → exhale → soften); auto-promoted when
     escalation risk is high.
   - **Scripts** — verbatim better lines.
   - **Replay** — a voiced, animated avatar replay of a kinder version of the exchange.

Success looks like: a distressed user leaves calmer and with concrete words; a reflective
user feels seen, not judged. Framing is **wellness awareness, never medical diagnosis**.

## Brand Personality

**Calm · wise · intentional** — grounded and quietly authoritative, with serene,
premium, high-craft restraint. A thoughtful companion who understands relationships
deeply, never preachy, never rushed. Every detail is deliberate; nothing feels cheap or
hurried. Bilingual warmth (Hinglish/Devanagari) is part of the voice, not a localization
afterthought.

**Positive references (the bar to be measured against):**
- **Calm / Headspace** — meditation-app serenity: generous space, slow breathing motion,
  reassuring tone. The closest category match for *feel*.
- **Duolingo / Finch** — friendly, character-led emotional design done *well* (the Sathi
  companion): approachable and encouraging without becoming childish or gamified-pushy.
- **Stripe / Linear** — not the look, the **craft bar**: pixel precision, buttery motion,
  immaculate component states. Feeling-first, but engineered impeccably.

## Anti-references

What Samvaad must **not** look or feel like:

- **Loud / hype consumer** — no neon gradients, aggressive CTAs, marketing shout,
  dark-pattern urgency, or streak/badge pressure.
- **Corporate SaaS dashboard** — no startup-blue, hero-metric template, dense KPI grids,
  or identical icon-heading-text card grids. This is not an analytics tool.
- **Clinical / medical** — no cold, diagnostic, hospital/therapy-portal tone; no "patient"
  framing or vitals-chart treatment. Wellness, not diagnosis.
- **The current dated visual crutches** — the existing brownish/ochre (`#C79A60`) palette
  and the heavy drop-shadow buttons read as dated to the owner. Evolve the visual identity
  toward a fresher, more serene/premium palette and softer, more modern depth (these are
  design-direction notes; resolve concretely in DESIGN.md, not here).

## Design Principles

1. **Emotion before insight.** Meet the user's state first — breathe when escalated, calm
   before data. The interface earns the right to be analytical only after it's been kind.
2. **Calm is craft, not emptiness.** Serenity comes from precision, spacing, and buttery
   motion (the Stripe/Linear bar) — not from removing everything. Restraint with intent.
3. **Non-judgmental by default.** Copy, scores, and states never shame. Easy exits, gentle
   language, "what you did well" before "what to try." Emotional safety is a hard rule.
4. **Bilingual as first-class voice.** Hinglish + Devanagari look intentional everywhere,
   never bolted on. The cultural reading lives in type, copy, and warmth.
5. **Consent-first, visible.** Privacy and consent (two-party for couples, auto-delete raw
   audio) are felt in the UI as care, not buried in legal fine print.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- **WCAG 2.2 AA** contrast and full keyboard navigation. Honor `prefers-reduced-motion`
  for the breathing/lotus and all reveal motion (the lotus animation already does).
- **Hinglish + Devanagari** render first-class — Latin + Devanagari (Noto Sans Devanagari)
  must look deliberate wherever text can be bilingual.
- **Low-end Android & slow networks.** India market: stay fast and legible on budget
  devices and patchy connections. Here, performance *is* an accessibility requirement.
- **Emotional safety.** Users may arrive distressed. Non-judgmental copy, easy exits, no
  shaming language; breathe-first when escalation is high.
