A wellness app that helps India's couples and individuals actually understand their hardest conversations, in their own language, with privacy by design.

In one line

Samvaad listens to a difficult conversation and gently shows people what happened, what they did well, and a kinder way to say it next time.

Not therapy. Not a chatbot. A calm, private, Hinglish-native companion that turns a tense moment into a moment of growth, in about a minute.

The problem

Relationships strain in silence

Most people never get neutral, affordable, in-the-moment help with how they communicate. Therapy carries stigma and cost. Self-help is generic, English-first, and Western in tone. And the hardest conversations happen in Hinglish, in homes, on commutes, late at night, where no tool meets them.

Stigma

Asking for help with a relationship still feels like admitting failure.

Cost & access

Counselling is expensive and scarce outside metros.

Wrong language

Existing apps don't speak Hinglish or understand Indian family dynamics.
The solution

How Samvaad works

Bring a conversation in.
Speak it, record it, or paste it. For a couple, for a relationship situation, or just for yourself.
Understand it.
A clear, non-blaming read: how connected the exchange was, what each person did well, and the patterns underneath.
Try something better.
Exact, ready-to-say lines in their own words, a one-minute breathing reset when things ran hot, and a voiced, animated replay of a kinder version of the same moment.
Keep going.
A gentle daily WhatsApp check-in and a growing picture of progress over time.
The experience

Three things, in the order a person needs them

Calm first

When a conversation ran hot, Samvaad leads with a 60-second breathing reset, because you can't hear feedback while your heart is racing. It only appears when it's actually needed.

Exact words to try

Not vague advice. Verbatim lines, in Hinglish, you could actually say next time, drawn from the moment you just had.

See it, kinder

A voiced, animated replay shows the same conversation, softened, so a better version isn't abstract, it's something you watch and hear.
Why we win

Affordable & habit-forming. A founding price of ₹199 and a once-a-day WhatsApp check-in that meets people where they already are.

A widening moat. Every session teaches the product which nudge fits whom, and when, a feedback loop competitors can't copy.
The opportunity

A large, underserved, mobile-first market

Hundreds of millions of young, smartphone-first Indians are coming of age as relationship-wellness and mental-health awareness rise sharply, while stigma around formal help remains. Samvaad sits at the intersection of three growing waves: digital wellness, vernacular-first products, and AI-native consumer apps.

Who

Urban & tier-2 couples and individuals, 22–40, comfortable in Hinglish, living on their phones.

When

The quiet moment after a fight; the commute debrief; the late-night journal.

Why now

Capable, low-cost AI + India-region infra make a private, affordable companion finally possible.

Trust & compliance

Built for India, by design

In an intimate category, trust isn't a checkbox, it's the brand. Samvaad is built to the India DPDP Act (2023) and 2025 Rules from day one: consent-first, purpose-limited, data resident in Mumbai, and framed as wellness awareness, never medical diagnosis.

"Your reflections are yours. We hold them gently, in India, and only ever show you your own."

What's next

The near-term milestones

  • Sign 10 founding, paying users and stand up the manual daily check-in loop.
  • Instrument everything with an internal KPI dashboard (conversion, retention, willingness-to-pay).
  • Prove the solo → couple conversion that defines the category's growth engine.
  • Then, and only then, scale distribution, automation, and the premium tiers.

The product is already rich and shipping. The single most important question now is the right one to answer first: will real people pay, and return? Everything in Phase A is built to answer it.
